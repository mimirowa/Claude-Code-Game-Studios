# Role Boundary Rules

- Producer coordinates; it does not invent specialist solutions.
- Design owns product behavior, not implementation architecture.
- Tech owns implementation contracts, not silent player-facing redesign.
- Art and Audio own their requirement packages only when impact exists.
- Implementer follows Work Order/Blueprint/Feature contracts and escalates
  invalid assumptions instead of changing specifications invisibly.
- QA judges conformance with approved criteria, not product desirability.
- Directors review only on configured triggers; no mandatory hierarchy hops.
- Executive Producer owns material product/scope/gate decisions, not routine
  writes, commits, pushes, or verification.
