# Retrospective

Use completed/failed Work Orders, cycle/blocker history, QA/Feedback, estimates,
commits, and gate results—not memory alone. Identify outcomes, surprises, causes,
what to preserve, and experiments. Separate system causes from individual blame.

Create a concise retrospective under `project/production/` with evidence,
2–5 owned actions, success measures, and review date. Route product discoveries
to Requirements/Features and significant architecture lessons to ADR/Blueprint.
Do not turn every observation into a new ceremony.
