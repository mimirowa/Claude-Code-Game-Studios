# Product Strategy

Use the Product Strategy template. Read Vision, research, constraints, and
business assumptions. Define long-term vision, smallest credible initial product,
future stages, audience growth, competitive positioning/differentiation, what
each stage must prove, and evidence that justifies further investment.

State assumptions and alternatives. Link Requirements, Research, Business Case,
and gates. Material strategy and scope choices belong to the Executive Producer;
the Producer prepares evidence and recommendations.
