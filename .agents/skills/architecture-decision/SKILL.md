# Architecture Decision

Confirm the decision warrants an ADR: cross-system impact, costly reversal,
important uncertainty, or durable constraint. Routine systems/local choices do
not. Allocate stable `ADR-NNN`; read linked Features, Blueprints, prior ADRs,
stack/budgets, evidence, and future multiplayer implications.

Use the ADR template to record context, drivers, real alternatives, decision,
rationale, consequences, risks, validation, and reversal/migration. Major ADRs
trigger Directors review; material product/scope tradeoffs return to Executive
Producer. Supersede rather than rewriting historical rationale.
