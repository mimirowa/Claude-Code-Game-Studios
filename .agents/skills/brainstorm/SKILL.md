# Brainstorm

Establish audience/problem, desired experience, constraints, commercial intent,
references, and uncertainties. Generate a small divergent set with explicit value,
differentiation, scope, risks, and what must be proven. Distinguish facts,
assumptions, and preferences.

Converge only after the Executive Producer chooses material direction. Route
unknowns to `$research` or `$prototype`; route selected intent to
`$product-vision`. Record discarded options/rationale when they prevent future
decision churn. Do not choose a stack prematurely or write a full GDD as a proxy
for evidence.
