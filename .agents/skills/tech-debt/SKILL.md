# Technical Debt

Inspect code, tests, Blueprints/ADRs, telemetry, incidents, and Work Orders.
Record evidence, affected artifacts/components, consequence, likelihood, trigger
for action, remediation options, estimate/risk, and verification. Distinguish
debt from missing features, bugs, speculative future-proofing, and style taste.

Route urgent contract violations through Feedback/Work Orders. Keep deferred debt
in canonical production tracking with stable identity and review trigger. Major
remediation architecture may need a Blueprint/ADR; routine cleanup does not.
