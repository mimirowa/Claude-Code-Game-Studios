# Product Vision

Read research, brainstorming, existing product artifacts, and Executive Producer
direction. Define target audience/problem, promise/value, differentiation,
experience principles/Core Pillars, boundaries/non-goals, platform/business
constraints, success signals, assumptions, and unresolved questions.

Offer meaningful alternatives for material direction and let the Executive
Producer decide. Persist the selected direction under `project/requirements/`
or a configured product root and link derived Requirements. Vision/Pillar changes
are Director-review triggers and require Executive Producer approval; ordinary
clarification and document maintenance do not.
