# Research

Create a stable `RES[-DOMAIN]-NNN` artifact from the Research template. Define
question, why it matters, scope/method, and stopping condition before gathering
evidence. Prefer primary/current sources; record source links, dates, and what
each supports. Separate finding, inference, conflicting evidence, confidence,
and recommendation.

Conclude with decision/experiment implications and links to affected Requirement,
Feature, or ADR. `INCONCLUSIVE` is valid. Never transform weak or conflicting
evidence into certainty. Research is an activity; no permanent research role or
external account action is implied.
