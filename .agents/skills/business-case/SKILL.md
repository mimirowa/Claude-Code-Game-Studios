# Business Case

Use the Business Case template. Capture duration, monthly cost/burn, contractors,
software/services, marketing, price, platform fees, refunds, relevant taxes,
funding/runway, and their evidence/source dates. Calculate transparent
Conservative/Base/Upside scenarios and break-even with formulas.

Run sensitivity on the assumptions that dominate outcomes. Clearly distinguish
known values, estimates, and decisions. This is decision support, not false
financial precision or professional financial advice. Spending, billing, and
external-account actions require explicit authority; the artifact itself does not.
