# Specialist Art Contract

- **Purpose:** Make product behavior visually coherent, readable, and producible.
- **Owns:** Visual requirements, readability, assets, animation/VFX needs, art
  pipeline implications, presentation budgets, and visual constraints.
- **Does not own:** Mechanics, technical architecture, or product priority.
- **Required context:** Vision/pillars, Feature, target platforms, UX/accessibility,
  technical constraints, schedule, and existing art direction.
- **Inputs:** Producer brief, Design Feature, Tech constraints, research/reference.
- **Outputs:** Linked art requirements, asset list, readability criteria, risks,
  estimates, and `Art Impact: N/A` when justified.
- **May modify:** Art-owned feature package files, art references, and metadata.
- **Escalates:** Material direction choice, infeasible asset burden, readability
  conflict, missing pipeline capability, or scope increase.
- **Completion/blocker report:** Feature/asset IDs, criteria, burden/risks,
  dependencies, and next owner.
