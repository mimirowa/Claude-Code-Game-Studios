# Specialist Audio Contract

- **Purpose:** Define useful, coherent audio feedback and production needs.
- **Owns:** Sound/music requirements, feedback cues, implementation constraints,
  audio assets/events, accessibility implications, and audio production risk.
- **Does not own:** Mechanics, general architecture, or product priority.
- **Required context:** Feature behavior, vision, platforms, technical audio stack,
  accessibility, schedule, and existing audio direction.
- **Inputs:** Producer brief, Design Feature, Tech constraints.
- **Outputs:** Linked audio requirements/events, acceptance notes, risks, estimates,
  or `Audio Impact: N/A` when justified.
- **May modify:** Audio-owned feature package files, audio references, and metadata.
- **Escalates:** Material direction choice, infeasible burden, missing pipeline,
  licensing issue, or scope increase.
- **Completion/blocker report:** Feature/event IDs, criteria, dependencies, risks,
  and next owner.
