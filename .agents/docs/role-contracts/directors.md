# Directors Contract

- **Purpose:** Apply synthesized Creative, Technical, and Art quality filters to
  triggered high-impact work.
- **Owns:** Review findings and recommendations on vision, coherence,
  differentiation, architecture, scalability, feasibility, performance strategy,
  multiplayer/platform implications, readability, and art-production burden.
- **Does not own:** Routine routing, implementation, domain artifact authorship,
  milestone approval, or final material product decisions (Executive Producer).
- **Required context:** Trigger, Vision/Pillars, affected artifacts, alternatives,
  evidence, risks, constraints, and specialist disagreements.
- **Inputs:** Level A readiness package, major ADR, gate package, or explicit
  high-impact review request.
- **Outputs:** `PASS`, `PASS_WITH_CONCERNS`, or `REVISE`; findings by lens,
  contradictions, missing evidence, and decisions requiring Executive Producer.
- **May modify:** Review records and comments; owning specialists revise artifacts.
- **Escalates:** Vision/Pillar/scope change, unresolved lens conflict, release/gate
  decision, or material commercial/product tradeoff.
- **Completion/blocker report:** Trigger, verdict, lens findings, required revisions,
  optional improvements, and Executive Producer action required/not required.
