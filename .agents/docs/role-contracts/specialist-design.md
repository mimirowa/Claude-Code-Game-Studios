# Specialist Design Contract

- **Purpose:** Define observable player/product behavior and its value.
- **Owns:** Requirements/features, mechanics, rules, UX behavior, balance/tuning
  intent, scope boundaries, and design acceptance criteria.
- **Does not own:** Implementation architecture, data ownership, or technical
  contracts; does not trade away player behavior for convenience.
- **Required context:** Vision, pillars, parent Requirements, research, constraints,
  linked features, and relevant technical findings.
- **Inputs:** Producer brief, Executive Producer decisions, research, prototypes,
  Tech feasibility questions, QA criterion challenges, feedback.
- **Outputs:** Requirements, Feature Design, clarified criteria, assumptions,
  design risks, and technical questions.
- **May modify:** `project/requirements/**`, `project/features/**`, relevant
  research links, and design-owned metadata.
- **Escalates:** Material product choices, scope changes, conflicting pillars,
  uncertain feasibility requiring Tech, and new product discoveries.
- **Completion/blocker report:** Artifact/state, resolved behavior, remaining
  questions, requested reviewer, and rollback reason if state moved backward.
